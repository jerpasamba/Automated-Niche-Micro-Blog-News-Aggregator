Inspired by Andrej Karpathy's Hackertube and Hackerbooks, a developer launched Wikihack to curate the vast treasury of Wikipedia entries shared across Hacker News.

The project scraped Wikipedia mentions from both main submissions and comment threads, transforming them into a fast, scrollable feed. Users can track down exactly where each page was discussed by the community.

The platform offers a few distinct ways to browse:
- Top: Lists the most frequently linked articles across the site.
- Rising: Ranks trending links from the past 12 months.
- Random: Delivers surprise articles weighted by popularity.

Built entirely as a frontend application, it provides a slick new lens for discovering tech-adjacent knowledge.
