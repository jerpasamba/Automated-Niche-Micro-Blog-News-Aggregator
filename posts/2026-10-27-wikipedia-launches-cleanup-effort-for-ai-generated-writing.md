Wikipedia contributors are tackling synthetic text across the platform. Through organized efforts like WikiProject AI Writing Cleanup, community volunteers are actively identifying and removing AI-generated prose from articles.

As automated writing tools become widespread, crowdsourced editorial oversight is emerging as a critical line of defense for maintaining information accuracy online.
