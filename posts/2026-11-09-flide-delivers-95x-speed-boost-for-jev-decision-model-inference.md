Inference engines tailored for standard LLMs don't always fit specialized decision frameworks. That is where Flide comes in, offering a purpose-built runtime environment crafted specifically for Jev-like decision models.

By focusing on exact inference, this new tool optimizes execution efficiency without sacrificing accuracy. It delivers impressive performance gains over general-purpose engines like vLLM and SGLang.

Key highlights include:
- Up to 9.5x speedup over standard inference solutions
- Native support for more than 30 popular local models
- Simple setup directly via Python's package manager

Developers looking to optimize local decision pipelines can test it out today.
