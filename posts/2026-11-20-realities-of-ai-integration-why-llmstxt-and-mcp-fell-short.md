Popular developer trends often promise massive gains, but real-world implementation can tell a different story. In a recent opinion piece, popular AI integration approaches like `llms.txt` files and Model Context Protocol (MCP) failed to make a meaningful difference for the team at Backpack for Laravel.

While these new formats aim to streamline context sharing for language models, adopting them did not yield the expected results. Instead, focusing on alternative practical solutions proved far more effective for their software workflow.

Key observations:
- Hype around new AI context protocols does not guarantee immediate ROI.
- Standard tooling tweaks often outperform newly adopted AI spec files.
