Can a single AI prompt render a complex work of literature over a six-hour span? A recent project put Opus 5.5 to the test, tasking the model with visualizing the book Invisible Cities in one shot.

The experiment showcases the potential of autonomous, long-running generative tasks:
- One comprehensive initial prompt
- Six continuous hours of processing
- Complete visual mapping of literary concepts

This single-shot experiment highlights how extended execution times and advanced models are expanding what creative AI tools can build autonomously.
