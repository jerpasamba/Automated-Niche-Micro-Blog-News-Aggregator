Autonomous AI agents are proving their worth far beyond standard software tasks. In an impressive display of automated research, a Google software engineer deployed a swarm of AI agents to crunch massive environmental datasets.

By directing 48 AI agents at NASA Lidar scans, the system sifted through complex terrain imagery at incredible speeds. The setup successfully identified an unmapped Maya pyramid after running for only 72 hours.

Key details of the project:
- Leveraged 48 autonomous AI agents working in tandem
- Analyzed heavy NASA Lidar topographic data
- Discovered ancient ruins in under three days

This experiment offers a fascinating glimpse into how multi-agent AI workflows can supercharge scientific discovery and archeological mapping.
