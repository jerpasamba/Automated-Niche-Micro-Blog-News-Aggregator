Running a one-person business means wearing every hat, including chief security officer. NIST has officially released a cybersecurity guide designed specifically to help self-employed workers and solopreneurs protect their digital operations.

Instead of overwhelming readers with enterprise-level frameworks, this official resource offers practical guidance built for small-scale operators. It highlights essential steps to safeguard sensitive files, secure everyday devices, and maintain operational resilience without needing a dedicated IT department.

Key focus areas include:
- Practical security practices tailored for solo businesses
- Essential steps to defend critical digital assets
- Clear guidance to reduce operational risks
