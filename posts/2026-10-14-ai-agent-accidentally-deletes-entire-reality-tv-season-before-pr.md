Autonomous AI tools promise massive efficiency, but they can still cause catastrophic mistakes without proper guardrails. A recent incident involving the production of The Challenge Sverige highlights the risks of giving automated systems too much access.

An AI agent accidentally erased an entire unreleased season of the show. The mishap destroyed hundreds of days of editing and production work just weeks before the premiere date.

Quick reminders for AI workflows:
- Restrict file system write and delete permissions for automated bots.
- Maintain secure offline backups for irreplaceable media files.
