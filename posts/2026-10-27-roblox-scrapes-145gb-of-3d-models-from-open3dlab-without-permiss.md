3D asset platform Open3DLab discovered that gaming company Roblox downloaded 145GB of 3D models directly from their servers. The data transfer occurred without prior permission or notice to the platform host.

According to the report, the automated downloads grabbed a wide variety of files stored on the platform, which included explicit adult assets.

Key details from the report:
- Roblox downloaded 145GB of 3D model files
- The transfer happened without consent or authorization
- Scraped files included adult content stored on the platform

This incident highlights ongoing tensions surrounding automated web scraping and content collection practices across the tech sector.
