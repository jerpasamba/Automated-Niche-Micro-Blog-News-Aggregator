AI agents are increasingly controlling desktop environments, but visual feedback can still feel limited. A new open-source utility solves this communication gap by giving software agents direct drawing access on your monitor.

Instead of relying solely on text logs, agents can now draw annotations directly over your active workspace. Key visual overlays include:

* Large directional arrows
* Bounding boxes around screen elements
* Custom text overlays

This makes human-in-the-loop workflows far clearer, letting you see exactly what your autonomous assistant is targeting in real time.
