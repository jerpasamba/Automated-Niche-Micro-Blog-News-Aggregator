Nostalgia is making a comeback with TitleArt, a fresh indie project recently launched on Hacker News. If you miss the bold, quirky text designs of the late nineties, this web tool brings those classic aesthetic vibes straight to your browser.

It offers a quick and easy way to turn plain text into WordArt-inspired retro graphics. It is a neat release for creators looking to add some vintage charm to their side projects or social media feeds.

Highlights of the tool:
- Generates text styled like it is 1997
- Fast and simple web-based interface
- Built for retro web enthusiasts
