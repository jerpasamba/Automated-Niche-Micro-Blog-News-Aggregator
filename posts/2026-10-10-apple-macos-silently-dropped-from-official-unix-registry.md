Apple operating system macOS has been silently removed from the official Unix register. For years, macOS held an official spot on the list of certified Unix systems, making this quiet change a notable update for the tech community.

The removal was spotted directly on the registry without any prior public announcement or explanation attached to the update.

Quick highlights:
* macOS no longer appears on the official Unix register
* The change was made quietly without official comment
