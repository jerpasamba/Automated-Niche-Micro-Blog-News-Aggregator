Artificial intelligence is shifting how engineering teams handle legacy code. As AI tools become more integrated into cybersecurity, older software architectures are facing unprecedented scrutiny.

Modern AI capabilities make it easier than ever to analyze code and locate longstanding vulnerabilities. This rapid evolution is compelling organizations to finally resolve security flaws that have lingered for up to 40 years.

Why this matters now:
- AI speeds up automated vulnerability discovery
- Legacy security flaws are exposed faster
- Decades of technical debt must finally be paid off

Updating legacy systems is no longer optional in an automated world.
