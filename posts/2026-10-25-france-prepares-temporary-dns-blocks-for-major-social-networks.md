Reports indicate that the French government has asked local internet service providers to prepare technical mechanisms for temporary DNS-level restrictions on major social media platforms.

The directive instructs ISPs to build the necessary technical capacity to restrict access to these popular channels on short notice. While intended to be temporary, the request reflects growing state-level involvement in managing digital traffic and online platforms.

Key details include:
* Local ISPs instructed to build rapid DNS blocking measures
* Focus on major, high-traffic social media networks
* System designed for temporary access intervention

This development highlights a continuing trend of regulatory bodies leveraging network infrastructure controls to respond to digital communication challenges.
