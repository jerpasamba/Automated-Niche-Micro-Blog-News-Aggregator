Formal proof verification is gaining momentum in the technology and mathematics communities. The open-source Lean theorem prover continues to play a central role in converting mathematical proofs into machine-checked code.

A recent update from the Xena Project highlights the progress of formalizing math in Lean. The discussion quickly caught traction online, generating over 100 points and active community discussion on Hacker News.

Key takeaways from the project:
* Machine-checked proofs offer full rigor for mathematical statements.
* Lean provides an open platform for digitizing logic.
* Automated proof checking bridges computer science and high-level mathematics.
