Lawyers in Brazil are facing court sanctions after embedding prompt injection instructions inside official legal filings. The move represents an aggressive attempt to trick automated AI systems that judges or opposition counsel might use to parse complex legal paperwork.

As AI tools become standard for summarizing lengthy files, adversarial prompt design is stepping out of security research and into real-world legal battles.

Why this matters:
- Adversaries can hide malicious instructions inside plain text documents.
- Judges view AI prompt tampering as bad-faith legal practice.
- Security auditing for document-summarizing tools is now essential.

This case sets a strong precedent for how legal systems will handle adversarial AI manipulation going forward.
