AI safety and alignment efforts are reaching into new fields. Anthropic recently convened meetings with religious scholars to discuss morality and values in artificial intelligence.

The consultations aim to bring diverse ethical and spiritual perspectives into the development of AI systems like Claude. These discussions highlight growing interest in:

- Exploring moral frameworks for AI behavior
- Gathering broader cultural perspectives on tech
- Integrating multidisciplinary guidance into model development

As AI tools scale globally, consulting experts beyond tech reflects a wider strategy to navigate complex ethical questions.
