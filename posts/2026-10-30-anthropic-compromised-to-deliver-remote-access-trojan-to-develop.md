A major security alert has rocked the AI ecosystem. Anthropic was compromised in an attack that delivered a remote access trojan directly to Claude developer laptops worldwide.

This incident underscores key challenges in modern AI development:
- Core AI vendors are becoming top targets for cyberattacks.
- Developer workstations face serious supply chain risks.
- Global dev networks allow threats to spread fast.

Anyone building in the Claude ecosystem should review their security setups and check for unexpected background activity.
