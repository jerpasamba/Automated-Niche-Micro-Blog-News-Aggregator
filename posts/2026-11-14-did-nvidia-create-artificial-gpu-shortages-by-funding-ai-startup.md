Reports indicate Nvidia might have strategically manufactured GPU demand by investing heavily in AI startups. Under this circular funding arrangement, funded companies allegedly used their capital injections primarily to acquire Nvidia hardware.

This practice may have artificially inflated market demand and created a perception of extreme chip shortages across the industry.

- Capital investments tied to purchasing Nvidia hardware
- Increased artificial pressure on global GPU supply
- Demand metrics driven by ecosystem reinvestments

If true, this strategy helped maintain high hardware prices while cementing market dominance.
