The Dutch financial institution ASN Bank has started blocking access for devices running custom, non-Google versions of Android. This policy directly impacts privacy enthusiasts who rely on alternative mobile operating systems, including the popular security-focused GrapheneOS.

The decision has sparked debate within the tech and open-source communities. Many users choose de-Googled operating systems specifically to avoid invasive tracking, but strict banking app compatibility checks are making it harder to maintain daily digital privacy.

Key takeaways from the report:
- ASN Bank now restricts banking app functionality on non-certified Android builds.
- Privacy-centric systems like GrapheneOS are officially impacted by the block.
